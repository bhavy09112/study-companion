Q

Explain the mechanism by which action potentials are propagated along a myelinated axon, and identify the key structures involved.

A

Action potentials propagate via saltatory conduction. The myelin sheath insulates the axon, preventing ion leakage. Depolarization only occurs at the Nodes of Ranvier, where voltage-gated Na+ channels are highly concentrated. This causes the signal to "jump" from node to node, significantly increasing conduction velocity and saving cellular energy.
